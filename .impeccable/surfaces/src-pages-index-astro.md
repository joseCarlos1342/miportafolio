## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a surveyor's topographic atlas of the systems José has built — his work rendered as mapped territory with contour linework, a graticule, a real legend, coordinates, and routes that connect skills to shipped sites. It refuses the two category defaults it would otherwise ship: the dark-terminal/neon dev portfolio and its opposite, the clean light-minimal card grid. It also refuses the AI-cluster look (warm cream + high-contrast display serif + signal-red, or neon-on-black) the incumbent skirted.

OWN-WORLD: A survey chart, not warm paper. Light = pale lichen chart ground (#E6E8E1) with deep warm-black-green ink (#17211C), two contour tints (lichen/buff), a hairline graticule, and ONE reserved accent: survey-overprint magenta (#C42B6B) for the active/committing signal only. Dark = night-chart ground (#0D1512) with luminous green contour lines and the same magenta, brighter. Type: Archivo (variable, expanded/heavy for map-title display and labels — a technical grotesque, deliberately not a display serif) + Spline Sans Mono used ONLY for real data (coordinates, elevations, stack specs, legend codes), never as decoration. Material signature: layered contour lines and a graticule grid drawn as vector/canvas, drawn survey glyphs (benchmark triangles, north arrow) as authored SVG in one stroke weight — never emoji or feTurbulence.

STORY: A recruiter or engineer lands inside a legible chart, immediately grasps "this person builds and ships real systems," follows routes from the legend (skills) to mapped sites (projects) each with a grid reference and evidence, and reaches the final waypoint to connect / download CV. Clarity beats spectacle: the map is the skin and the wayfinding, never a puzzle; the reading order stays a clear top-to-bottom narrative.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed living contour field (animated 2D canvas, degrades to static SVG). Over it, a survey title block: "JOSÉ CARLOS GÓMEZ R." set large in Archivo expanded caps like a chart title, a one-line role/tagline, a mono coordinate/graticule reference, and a benchmark marker. Primary action (View projects) as the magenta commit control; LinkedIn and CV as secondary survey controls. No eyebrow/kicker above the title (hard ban).

FORM: Cartographic system atlas (a surveyor's topographic/nautical chart).
